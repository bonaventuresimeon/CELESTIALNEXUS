# Design and business system

## Brand hierarchy
Supplied direction: Simeon & Partners → VESPER → CELESTIALNEXUS → JOAN. Use “CELESTIALNEXUS — a VESPER venture.” Do not infer incorporation or registered ownership. JOAN is an intelligence engine, not a separate company.

## Visual system
Background #080D13; surfaces #111922; gold #CFB07A; foreground #F3F2EE; secondary text #A5AFB9; borders #27303A. Emerald is reserved for a verified completed website state, not live trading health. System sans-serif typography keeps pages fast and readable. H1 36–72px, H2 30–46px; primary body 16px; metadata is smaller. Use 5–6% page gutters, 18–28px component gaps, restrained 3–7px radii and thin borders. Keep decorative glow low contrast and behind content. Functional geometry and charts use SVG; provided brand images remain unaltered except for web optimization.

## Components
Header/footer, buttons, product cards, badges, tabs, searchable docs, status rows, roadmap, enquiry form, architecture, pipeline and illustrative chart. Reuse tokens for future ventures; replace only brand assets and product content. No invented testimonials or metric counters. Existing dashboard screenshot was not supplied: dashboard fidelity is pending, and the concept must not be labelled product evidence.

## Motion and imagery
Purposeful hover transitions; reduced-motion override. No flashing, particles or continuous animation. The original celestial artwork anchors the hero, JOAN anchors the AI narrative, and the full brand hierarchy appears on About. Favicon is a compact CN monogram; supplied artwork is the app icon and social preview source. A future official logo system should use owner-approved vector masters. No trademark status is asserted.

## Social template
Use `/brand/celestial.webp` as the artwork source. Pair a short gold headline with a near-black panel; preserve the emblem. Open Graph uses the supplied square brand image. Future campaign variants should state capability status, avoid financial performance claims and link to evidence. Do not imply social accounts exist until verified.

## Positioning
Product: intelligence and infrastructure across Data → Intelligence → AI → Risk → Strategy → Execution → Analytics. Enterprise: inspectable decision context, integration requirements and separate risk controls. AWS: workload rationale and design choices, without asserting deployments or partnership. Investor: a platform thesis awaiting verified traction and product evidence.

## Investor presentation structure
1. Venture and legal relationship (verified)
2. Fragmented workflow problem
3. Connected intelligence solution
4. Product evidence with explicit status
5. Market research and source methodology
6. Architecture and risk boundary
7. AI evaluation and responsible deployment
8. Competitive comparison supported by evidence
9. Business model assumptions
10. Validated traction, if available
11. Roadmap and milestones
12. Team biographies (verified)
13. Financial assumptions / use of funds (private)
14. Risks and next steps
Never fill missing slides with invented figures.

## Launch dependencies
Approved dashboard screenshots, repository evidence, founder biography, incorporation/ownership details, business/security email, registered address if applicable, genuine social links, pricing decisions and legal review. Define support ownership and enquiry retention. Public launch should wait for these operational decisions; the initial Site remains owner-private.
